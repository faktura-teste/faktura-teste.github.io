Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FAKTURA (“we”, “our”) handles information in connection with this website and our internal content publishing tools, including FAKTURA Publisher.

1. About FAKTURA

FAKTURA is an independent bilingual food media project that publishes original recipes, cooking tips and food-related editorial content. FAKTURA Publisher is intended for managing content on our own social media accounts.

2. Information we process

This static website does not provide user registration, contact forms, or direct payment functionality. When visiting the site, technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata may be processed by our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, in accordance with its policies.

If we connect FAKTURA Publisher to a social media platform through an authorized API, the tool may process account identifiers, content, boards, Pins, publishing metadata and analytics necessary for its authorized functions.

3. How information is used

We use information to maintain the website, prepare and publish our own editorial content, manage our own social media presence, review publishing results and maintain the security of our internal tools.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Social media interactions and API access are also subject to the respective platform's privacy terms. Information may be processed by service providers only as necessary to provide the relevant functionality.

5. Storage and security

We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards for internal publishing tools and API credentials. We retain information only for as long as needed for the stated purposes or applicable obligations.

6. Your choices and requests

For questions about information processed by FAKTURA, please contact us through our official Instagram account. Requests relating to data held directly by Pinterest, Instagram or GitHub should also be addressed to those services.

7. Children

Our website and publishing tools are not designed to collect personal information from children.

8. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website, tools or legal requirements. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
