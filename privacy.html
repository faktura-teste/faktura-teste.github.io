Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FAKTURA (“we”, “our”), an independent bilingual food media project, handles information associated with our website and the FAKTURA Publisher internal content workflow, including any authorized Pinterest API integration.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

FAKTURA publishes original recipes, food photography, cooking tips and editorial stories in Russian and English. FAKTURA Publisher is a private workflow used by the project operator to prepare, review and, when authorized, publish content to FAKTURA's own social media accounts. It is not a service offered to third-party Pinterest users.

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about FAKTURA Publisher, contact fakturaru@outlook.com.

2. Website visitors

Our public website is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not offer user registration, payment processing or a contact form. FAKTURA does not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts to these pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own policies. See GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us, we process your email address and message to respond to your inquiry.

3. Pinterest API information and permissions

If Pinterest approves API access and the project operator authorizes the connection, FAKTURA Publisher may request only the permissions necessary to manage content on our own Pinterest business account. Depending on the approved permissions and enabled functions, API responses may include account identifiers, our boards and their identifiers, Pins and their identifiers, publication status, and relevant account information.

We use these data only to identify an authorized board, prepare or submit explicitly approved Pins, and check the results of those actions. We do not access or manage other people's Pinterest accounts. We do not collect Pinterest passwords, session cookies or other users' login credentials.

All Pin publishing decisions remain subject to a deliberate review and approval step by the account operator. Merely preparing a draft does not authorize publishing.

4. How information is handled and retained

FAKTURA may store its own original photographs, editorial drafts, captions, publishing instructions and internal approval records in its local content-management system. These materials are created by FAKTURA, not obtained from Pinterest API responses.

Information retrieved from Pinterest through the API is used only as needed for the authorized operation and is not retained as a permanent copy, except where Pinterest expressly permits retention. Any temporary handling is limited to the relevant request and operation. Authorized API tokens, if configured, are treated as confidential credentials and are not placed in public website files or repositories.

We keep email correspondence and internally created content only as long as reasonably needed for the corresponding purpose or an applicable legal obligation.

5. Disclosure and service providers

We do not sell personal information or Pinterest API data, and we do not share Pinterest API data with advertising networks, data brokers or unrelated third parties. Information may be exchanged with Pinterest when we make authorized API requests. GitHub Pages hosts the public website; our internal publishing workflow is locally hosted. Other providers, if used for an authorized function, are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to limit unauthorized access to internal publishing tools and API credentials. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices, access and deletion requests

To ask about information processed directly by FAKTURA, request a correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern, email fakturaru@outlook.com. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

The Pinterest account operator can withdraw the app's authorization using Pinterest's connected-app or account settings, where available. After authorization is revoked, FAKTURA Publisher will no longer be able to use that authorization for API requests. Requests about data held directly by Pinterest should be directed to Pinterest under its own privacy policy.

8. Children

Our website and internal publishing tool are not directed to children and are not designed to collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, internal tools, applicable requirements or platform integrations change. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date on this page.
